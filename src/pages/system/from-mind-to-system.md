<div class="card" id="core-idea">
  <h2>Core Idea</h2>
  <p>At a certain point, the work stops being only about understanding the mind. It becomes about shaping the conditions that influence how the mind operates. Psychology explains what is happening. This layer asks: <strong>what makes the better pattern easier to fall into?</strong></p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="the-shift">
  <h2>The Shift</h2>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>From explanation to adjustment.</strong> Not just "how does the mind work?" but "what makes it easier for the mind to work better in real situations?"</li>
    <li><strong>From after to during.</strong> Catching things while they happen, not only after they go wrong.</li>
    <li><strong>From discipline to conditions.</strong> Change what is easy by default, and behavior follows.</li>
    <li><strong>From internal to shared.</strong> Some of the thinking can live outside of you, in tools and structures.</li>
    <li><strong>From outcomes to generators.</strong> Stop fixing each result. Shape what keeps producing it.</li>
  </ol>
</div>

<div class="card" id="why-it-matters">
  <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
  <p>Understanding alone does not change much. When the structures around you support the direction you want, you stop having to push everything forward manually. There is more consistency, less friction, fewer unnecessary decisions.</p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="practice">
  <h2>Practice</h2>
  <p>When something keeps going wrong, stop asking "why did I do that?" and ask instead:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>What around me is shaping this behavior?</li>
    <li>What shows up by default in this moment?</li>
    <li>What would make the better pattern easier to repeat?</li>
    <li>What can live outside my head instead of inside it?</li>
  </ul>
</div>
