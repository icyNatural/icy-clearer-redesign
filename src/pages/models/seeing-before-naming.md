<div class="card" id="core-idea">
  <h2>Core Idea</h2>
  <p>Most people do not fully see things. They name them, then move on. "Chair." "Problem." "Annoying." The label helps you move fast, but it also cuts off everything else that is there. This model is about letting the experience arrive before the label does.</p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="the-process">
  <h2>The Process</h2>
  <p>The process of seeing before naming follows a clear sequence:</p>
  <ol>
      <li><strong>Contact:</strong> First, make direct contact with the thing itself.</li>
      <li><strong>Detail:</strong> Observe its specific, concrete details.</li>
      <li><strong>Structure:</strong> Understand how the parts relate to each other.</li>
      <li><strong>Label:</strong> Only then, apply a name or concept.</li>
  </ol>
</div>

<div class="card" id="why-it-matters">
    <h2>Why It Matters</h2>
    <p>When a label comes too early, observation stops, detail disappears, and everything becomes "already known." When perception comes first, detail returns, differences become visible, and understanding becomes deeper.</p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="failure-mode">
    <h2>Common Failure Mode</h2>
    <strong>Naming Replaces Observation</strong>
    <p>The label is treated as the thing itself. The map replaces the territory. Once named, the object is filed away, and direct perception ceases. This is efficient, but costly.</p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="questions-to-ask">
    <h2>Questions to Ask</h2>
    <strong>Use these to break a pattern:</strong>
    <ul>
        <li>What am I <em>actually</em> seeing?</li>
        <li>What details disappeared right after I named it?</li>
        <li>What have I assumed here?</li>
        <li>If I had no word for this, what would I see?</li>
    </ul>
</div>

<div class="card" id="practice">
    <h2>Practice</h2>
    <p>Pick any object in your environment. For five seconds, do not name it. Just notice:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>shape & form</li>
        <li>texture & material</li>
        <li>light & shadow</li>
        <li>position & relation</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Then, name it. Feel the shift from full-spectrum perception to a single concept. That is the difference.</p>
</div>
