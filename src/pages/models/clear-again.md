<div class="card" id="core-idea">
  <h2>Core Idea</h2>
  <p>Clarity is not a permanent state to be held, but a place you learn to return to. You will drift. Pressure, noise, and intensity change how you see things. That is normal. The goal is not to achieve a perfect, unbroken state of clarity. The goal is to become skilled at returning to clarity, again and again.</p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="the-drift">
  <h2>What Drift Feels Like</h2>
  <p>When your state shifts, your perception shifts with it. You are still functioning, but you are not aligned. Signs of drift include:</p>
  <ul>
      <li>Small things feel bigger or more urgent than they are.</li>
      <li>Attention becomes scattered and easily pulled.</li>
      <li>Decisions feel harder or more confusing.</li>
      <li>Reactions happen faster than awareness.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="card" id="the-shift">
    <h2>The Shift</h2>
    <p>From chasing a state to trusting a process. Instead of thinking: <em>"I need to hold on and stay clear."</em></p>
    <p>You start thinking: <em>"I've drifted. I know how to get clear again."</em></p>
    <p>This shift from fear to trust is what creates stability over time.</p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="practice">
    <h2>Practice: How to Get Clear</h2>
    <p>You don't solve the problem. You create space so the solution can appear. <strong>Simple Resets:</strong></p>
    <ul>
        <li><strong>Reduce Input:</strong> Close tabs, turn off notifications, find a quiet space.</li>
        <li><strong>Slow Down:</strong> Intentionally move and speak at 75% of your normal speed.</li>
        <li><strong>Move Your Body:</strong> Walk, stretch, change your posture.</li>
        <li><strong>Focus on One Thing:</strong> Pick a single, simple, physical task.</li>
    </ul>
</div>

<div class="card" id="questions-to-ask">
    <h2>Questions to Ask</h2>
    <strong>When something feels off, ask:</strong>
    <ul>
        <li>Am I clear right now?</li>
        <li>What changed in my state or environment?</li>
        <li>What is the simplest way to create space?</li>
    </ul>
</div>

<div class="card" id="failure-mode">
    <h2>Common Failure Mode</h2>
    <strong>Solving from a Drifted State</strong>
    <p>The most common error is trying to analyze, fix, or push through a problem while your perception is still distorted. This amplifies confusion and leads to poor decisions. The rule is: <strong>Clear first, then decide.</strong></p>
</div>
