<div class="card" id="core-idea">
  <h2>Core Idea</h2>
  <p>Instead of judging actions as 'good' or 'bad,' ask: does this help things hold together (coherence) or make them fall apart (entropy)? This model is for reading what an action <em>does</em> after it enters a system. It is not for labeling people. A person can create stability in one moment and disruption in another. The point is to notice what the action leads to.</p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="coherence">
  <h2>Coherence</h2>
  <p>An action creates coherence when it makes the system clearer, steadier, or easier to repair.</p>
  <p><strong>Coherence Increases:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>Clarity & Understanding</li>
    <li>Trust & Alignment</li>
    <li>Future Stability</li>
    <li>Ease of Repair</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Coherence does not always feel soft. A direct, uncomfortable conversation creates coherence if it removes critical confusion.</p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="entropy">
  <h2>Entropy</h2>
  <p>An action creates entropy when it makes the system harder to hold together.</p>
  <p><strong>Entropy Increases:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>Confusion & Noise</li>
    <li>Tension & Distrust</li>
    <li>Unnecessary Costs</li>
    <li>Delayed Repair</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Entropy does not always look dramatic. A small avoidance or a soft lie that preserves immediate comfort can create massive entropy by leaving the real issue unresolved.</p>
</div>

<div class="card" id="questions-to-ask">
    <h2>How to Read the Direction</h2>
    <strong>After an action, ask:</strong>
    <ul>
        <li>Did this reduce confusion or increase it?</li>
        <li>Did this restore trust or weaken it?</li>
        <li>Did this make the next step clearer or harder?</li>
        <li>Did this lower the future cost or raise it?</li>
    </ul>
</div>

<div class="card" id="failure-mode">
    <h2>Common Failure Mode</h2>
    <strong>Judging People, Not Actions</strong>
    <p>This model is a tool for diagnosis, not a weapon for judgment. Using it to label a person as "chaotic" or "an entropy-creator" is a failure. That is a story, not a system analysis. The correct response is to name the effect, reduce the damage, and if needed, adjust the system to prevent it from happening again.</p>
</div>
